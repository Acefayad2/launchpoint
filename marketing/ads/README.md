## First-draft specifications

- 1080 × 1920 portrait, 30 fps, 20 seconds.
- H.264 video, AAC audio, yuv420p MP4.
- Captions checked against the authored script with Whisper word timing; full script coverage verified.
- Decoding, video/audio duration, and sample caption frames verified for all three exports.
- Generated business scenes and illustrative UI concepts are not client testimonials or proof of delivered projects.
- Use the matching [post copy](post-copy.md). CTA uses direct messages rather than the website form.

Higgsfield Seedance 2.5 supplied cinematic source footage and native audio. Higgsedit supplied controlled typography, concept interfaces, and end cards. The Higgsfield subtitles pipeline supplied verified caption timing and burning. Generated screen text was replaced in the final edits.
