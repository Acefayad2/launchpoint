A few things to know

Questions before we launch?

Straight answers to what owners ask us first. Anything else, ask it in your quote request.

Ask us directly →
What can LaunchPoint help with?

Websites, local search, Google Ads, review cards, photography, social media, and connected client systems. We can focus on one piece or put together a broader project.

How much will my project cost?

Each project starts with a quote based on what you need. We outline the deliverables, timeline, and any ongoing services before work begins.

How long does it take to launch?

Timing depends on the scope, content, and integrations. We'll agree on a realistic schedule with you at the start and keep you informed along the way.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current setup and recommend whether to improve it, connect new tools, or rebuild the parts that are holding you back.

What do you need from me?

Tell us about your business, your goals, and what is not working today. If you have a logo, brand materials, or an existing website, those are helpful starting points.
